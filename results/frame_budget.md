# Frame drop model, architecture A3

ESTIMATES, not measurements: see the script headers. Old design for comparison (`results/tpu_budget_by_category.md`, TPU in the FPGA at 713,840 clocks/frame): 20 of 100 frames exceed one frame period (clutter 7, noise 6, dark 3, grad 2, shadow 2).
